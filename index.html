// HOW TO PLAY — PumaSOC
OBJECTIVE

Survive 7 game days as a SOC operator. Defend company infrastructure against nation-state and criminal threat actors. You lose if 3 breaches succeed.

THE THREAT LOOP

Every few minutes a threat actor attempts an intrusion. It is checked against your Coverage %:

  • Detected → CRIT fires in SIEM, incident created — assign an analyst immediately.
  • Undetected → silent dwell. Surfaced eventually by your MTTD timer — or not at all until breach.
  • Breach → undetected dwell expires, or a detected incident goes unhandled too long.
RESPONDING TO INCIDENTS
  1. CRIT alert fires in the SIEM strip at the bottom.
  2. Incident card appears in the centre panel with a running dwell timer. An unassigned card turns amber, then red, as the unhandled-breach window runs out.
  3. Click ASSIGN ANALYST — a free analyst begins working it.
  4. Analyst works through: TRIAGE → CONTAIN → ERADICATE → RECOVER
  5. On RECOVER completing, the analyst is freed and the incident bonus paid immediately. A LESSONS LEARNED sign-off lingers for 2h — click it for an optional +1% IR speed bonus in that category (stacks up to 25%). Nothing is lost if you skip it.
KEY STATS
  • Coverage % — detection chance on spawn. Your primary defence. Target 50%+ immediately.
  • MTTD — Mean Time to Detect: the average time before an undetected threat is surfaced. If it stays close to the breach window, lurkers detonate before you find them. Rules and intel feeds drive it down.
  • Vuln Score — directly increases threat spawn frequency. ALL unpatched vulns count, including ones you haven't scanned for — unknown exposure is still exposure. Scan to reveal what to patch.
  • Analysts Free — your throughput ceiling. Running 0 free while incidents pile up is the most common way to lose.
  • Budget — MSSP/SAT run on 7-day contracts you must renew, and consultant engagements lapse after 7 days. Idle money doesn't defend you. Spend it.
RANDOM EVENTS

Crises fire periodically — DDoS barrages, zero-days, tool outages, insider threats, budget audits. Shown as ACTIVE EVENTS in the left panel. Events may cut Coverage %, drain budget, or spike threat rates. Ride them out.

NETWORK MAP

The minimap in the left panel shows your infrastructure in real time. Green = healthy. Amber = vulnerable (unpatched CVEs). Red = under attack or breached. Animated packets show active threats moving through the network.

DETECTION & INTELLIGENCE
DETECTION ENG — $25k–$50k per upgrade Upgrade SIEM detection rules across all 12 MITRE ATT&CK technique categories (up to level 5 each). Each level raises Coverage % and adds a category-specific detection bonus. Your highest-ROI investment — prioritise this above almost everything else.
THREAT INTEL — $30k–$190k per feed Subscribe to commercial threat intel feeds for passive Coverage % boosts and MTTD reductions. Each feed targets specific actor categories and stacks with Detection Engineering. Buy the cheapest feeds first — ROI diminishes as you stack them.
VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT
VULN MGMT — $15k scan, variable patch costs Run a scan to discover CVEs across your infrastructure. Patch individually (CRIT: $15–25k, HIGH: $8–12k, MED: $3–6k, LOW: $1–3k), patch CRIT/HIGH at a discount, or patch everything with a 15% bulk discount. Vuln Score counts every unpatched vuln — discovered or not — and directly accelerates threat spawn rate.
AUTOMATED RESPONSE
SOAR PLATFORM — $100k platform + $20k per playbook Security Orchestration, Automation and Response. The platform alone does nothing — deploy playbooks per ATT&CK category ($20k each, 12 total). Each playbook adds +25% speed to TRIAGE and CONTAIN stages for that technique. Best purchased mid-game when incidents are overwhelming analysts.
ADVERSARIAL TESTING
RED TEAM — $30k–$150k per engagement Commission a penetration test. On completion, findings permanently close detection gaps (+2–6% coverage, up to +10% total), discover hidden vulns, and suppress an attack category related to the engagement type for 48h — threats in it auto-detect. The Full Red Team Engagement targets whichever category your detection rules are weakest in, for 72h.
ANALYST STAFFING
HIRE ANALYST — $75k Each analyst handles one active incident at a time. Maximum 8. Hire early — analysts are your throughput ceiling and the most common bottleneck.
ANALYST ACTIVITIES (consume one slot for the duration)
SEND TO TRAINING — $50k · 24h · permanent +10% IR speed (max +30%) Offline for 24 game-hours. On return, permanently boosts all analysts' IR stage speed by +10%. Three trainings caps the bonus. Do it early before incident waves hit.
THREAT HUNT — $60k · 12h · surfaces 1–3 hidden threats Proactive hunt for lurking undetected threats. On return, creates incidents for any found — turning silent dwell into cases you can close. Use when MTTD is high and you suspect threats are accumulating invisibly.
TABLETOP EXERCISE — $25k · 8h · +15% IR speed for 48h IR team runs through a simulated scenario. On completion, grants a temporary +15% speed boost lasting 48 game-hours — visible in the posture panel. Stack with training bonus for maximum throughput during a surge.
PATCH SPRINT — $25k · 8h · free-patches all LOW vulns Analyst focuses entirely on remediation. On completion, patches every discovered LOW-severity vulnerability at no additional cost. Only available when low vulns are outstanding. LOW vulns don't feed Vuln Score, so this is a grading clean-up (the S/A grades count every unpatched vuln), not a spawn-rate lever.
OSINT RESEARCH — $40k · 12h · 3 guaranteed-detection charges Analyst profiles active threat actors. On completion, loads 3 charges — next 3 threats that spawn are immediately detected regardless of Coverage %, each logged with OSINT attribution in SIEM. Use before anticipated high-activity periods.
PARTNERS
SECURITY AWARENESS TRAINING — $20k–$100k for a 7-day term, cheaper renewals Three tiers blocking 25%–60% of Initial Access attempts at the employee layer — the threat never enters your environment. Initial Access is the most common technique in the wild, so this bites. Higher tiers also add up to +10% detection coverage. Renew before the 7-day term lapses.
MSSP — $100k–$350k for a 7-day contract, $10k–$50k renewals Three tiers: Alert Monitoring (auto-assign incidents), Co-Managed SOC (+ auto-sign lessons learned, +10% IR speed), Fully Managed SOC (+ overflow analyst capacity, +15% IR speed). A safety net that escalates from triage assist to full co-management. Only worth it when you're consistently running out of free analysts.
CONSULTANT — $75k engagement fee Passive Coverage % and MTTD improvement plus a daily briefing with one data-driven action recommendation tailored to your current posture. Hire on day 1 — the briefings alone direct your spending effectively.

Survive a week and you can extend the engagement. Your budget, analysts, and SOAR platform carry over. Everything else is subject to the natural entropy of a real security program.

FIRST MOVES BY DIFFICULTY
ANALYST (Easy) — $800k start, 3 analysts, 45% coverage Hire a Consultant day 1 for guided spending. Push Coverage above 60% fast. Buy Security Awareness Training early — it pays for itself within 2 income cycles. Run a Tabletop once your team is stable.
ENGINEER (Medium) — $500k start, 2 analysts, 25% coverage Hire a third analyst immediately. Send one to training as soon as coverage is above 40%. Buy SAT before the incident wave peaks around day 2. OSINT research is strong here — 12h is affordable when you have 3 analysts.
HUNTER (Hard) — $300k start, 1 analyst, 10% coverage First $75k goes on a second analyst — no exceptions. Patch sprints and OSINT are your best early tools. Don't buy SOAR or MSSP until coverage is above 40%. Every dollar counts; avoid analyst activities until you have 3+ on the team.
GENERAL STRATEGY
  • Coverage first. Everything else is secondary until you can detect most threats on spawn.
  • Never let analysts sit idle when incidents are open. Unhandled incidents breach. If all incidents are covered and a slot is free, run an analyst activity.
  • Watch the dwell timers. An unassigned incident breaches when its dwell reaches the unhandled window — the card goes red at 70%. Triage fast, especially on hard difficulties.
  • Campaign actors escalate. The same actor appearing multiple times gains skill and speed. Don't ignore repeated names in the SIEM.
  • Vuln Score compounds. Every point above 50 meaningfully increases spawn rate. Patch regularly — not in bulk emergencies.
  • Budget is a weapon, not a score. Idle money doesn't defend you. Invest aggressively early.
SCORING

Your final grade is determined by posture at end-of-operation: breaches are a hard floor (3 = F, 2 = D, 1 = C). For zero-breach runs, grade is based on Coverage %, MTTD, unpatched vulnerabilities, and open incidents at game end (S / A / B).
